"What did he say?" I ask Peeta. For the first time I look at him and see how the fake flames make him shine.

And I must look just the same.

"I think he said we should hold hands," says Peeta. He takes my right hand with his left and we look to Cinna for confirmation. He nods and gives a thumbs up, and that is the last thing I see before we ride out into the city.

The initial panic that grips the crowd at our appearance quickly turns into cheers and cries of "District 12!" Everyone turns toward us, ignoring the three wagons ahead. For a moment I am frozen, but then I spot us on a giant screen and am speechless, for we simply look breathtaking. In the falling dusk, the fire illuminates our faces.

We look as if our flowing capes are trailing a train of fire behind us. With the sparing makeup, Cinna was right, we both look better and are still very recognizable.

<em>Remember, heads up. Smile. They will love you!</em>

Cinna's voice echoes in my head. I lift my chin a little higher, put on my most winning smile, and wave with my free hand.
