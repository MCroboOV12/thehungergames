The story takes place in a dark future in Panem — a land with the Capitol and twelve districts. Each year the districts must send one boy and one girl to the so-called Hunger Games as punishment for a past rebellion. These tributes have to fight each other to the death in an arena.

16-year-old Katniss Everdeen from District 12 volunteers when her little sister Primrose is chosen at the Reaping. Together with the boy Peeta Mellark she is taken to the Capitol. Her mentor Haymitch Abernathy helps them prepare.

In the arena Katniss fights not only the other tributes but also dangerous traps built by the Game Makers. She allies with the young Rue from District 11 and tries to survive. In the end only Katniss and Peeta remain — and they threaten to commit suicide together so the Game Makers will let them both win.
